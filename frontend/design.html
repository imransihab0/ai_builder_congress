2. User Journey & Wireframe Architecture

1

Step 1: Multimodal Farmer Intake (Voice / Text / Leaf Photo)

Farmer provides location (e.g. Bogura), land acreage (2 acres), and capital budget (80,000 BDT), or snaps a leaf picture of diseased paddy.

2

Step 2: 3D Digital Twin Grounding & Geospatial Alignment

Three.js WebGL terrain aligns coordinates with SRDI Soil Texture and BRRI/IRRI Alternate Wetting & Drying water grids.

3

Step 3: Deterministic Financial Evaluation & Season Calendar

Zero-hallucination math engine generates a 3-crop comparison table and dated BARC FRG 2024 fertilizer schedule.

4

Step 4: 1-Click bdapps Telecom CaaS Checkout & Dial 16123

Inline action chips [[BUTTON:...]] allow direct fertilizer bulk reservation and direct hotline calling to agricultural extension officers.

3. Component Hierarchy & Micro-Interactions

Three.js 3D Digital Twin

Interactive icosahedron terrain sphere with real-time wireframe vertices, smooth scroll damping, and dynamic layer toggles (Soil Texture, AWD, Vegetation Index).

Live Agent Workbench

Real-time DAG status pills, execution trace logging with millisecond timestamps, and visual tool dispatch cards.

Actionable Token Chips

Custom token parser regex renders interactive checkout buttons and tel: dialer links directly in conversational threads.
